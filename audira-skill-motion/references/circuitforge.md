## Signature visual and narrative grammar

- **Authentic PCB Geometry:** Dark matte green solder mask with octilinear 0°/45°/90° copper routing, ENIG gold vias, and white monoline silkscreen text.
- **Luminous Current Propagation:** Amber DC power pulses and Cyan high-frequency clock signals physically race along the traces toward the BGA die.
- **Physical Macro Depth:** Realistic 3D relief with raised solder pads, ceramic SMD decoupling capacitors, and reflective silicon heat spreaders.
- **Hardware Diagnostic HUD:** Engineering telemetry including core frequency (4.8 GHz), operating voltage (1.15V DC), power draw, and pinout indicators.

## Act structure that works (8–20s films)

1. **Power Arrival (0–25%):** Macro camera tracks closely along a copper trace as the first amber power pulse races toward the center; power LED illuminates.

3. **Motherboard Overview (65–100%):** Camera pulls back into a clean top-down technical view; the entire circuit bus network operates in harmony; telemetry locks into stable operating specs.
