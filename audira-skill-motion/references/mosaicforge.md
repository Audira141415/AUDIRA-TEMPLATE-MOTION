## Visual Guardrails
1. Keep 9:16 safe zones (top 180 px / bottom 380 px at 1080×1920); the overhead reveal must keep the subject in the middle band.
2. The picture must read from above in one glance: bold silhouette, dark outline row, limited palette.
3. Gold is reflective metal, never yellow paint; keep bloom low (threshold ≥ 0.9) so tiles stay crisp.
4. Pass the Mute Test: the tesserae counter and the rise to overhead tell the story without audio.
5. Original imagery only; do not reproduce famous mosaics, coats of arms or logos.
