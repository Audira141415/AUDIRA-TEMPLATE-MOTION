## Signature visual and narrative grammar

- **Physical Subsurface Transmission & Refraction:** Frosted glass geometries using physical transmission ($IOR \approx 1.50$), simulating real-world optical refraction where background elements distort gracefully through the glass slab.
- **Prismatic Edge Dispersion:** Beveled borders reflect split spectrum hues (cyan, magenta, amber, emerald), capturing how real crystal splits white light into spectral rainbows.
- **Zero-Gravity Buoyancy & Wobble:** Liquid orbs and cards float with natural harmonic oscillations, breathing with gentle surface tension elasticity rather than rigid robotic snapping.
- **Specular Obsidian Floor:** A dark, mirror-sheen obsidian ground reflects high-key softbox illumination and colored rim lights.
- **Translucent Spatial UI Overlays:** Frosted pill badges with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and semi-transparent specular borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
