## Which briefs belong here?

Automatically select **UKIYOFORGE** for briefs involving:
- "Sejarah atau budaya Jepang, era Edo, cerita samudra dan nelayan"
- "Tema laut, tsunami (edukasi), ombak, pelayaran, kekuatan alam"
- "Visual seni cetak klasik yang elegan, poster seni, estetika Asia Timur"
- "Konten seni dan desain: teknik cetak, warna datar, garis ukir"

Do **not** pick it for Chinese ink wash (use INKSTORY) or for Indonesian heritage (use WAYANGFORGE or MANUSCRIPT).

Describe the look in friendly Indonesian:
> *"Gaya cetak kayu Jepang (ukiyo-e) yang dibangun dari lapisan kertas 3D: ombak bergulung dengan buih seperti cakar, gunung kembar, perahu nelayan, matahari merah terbit, warna datar biru Prusia, merah vermilion dan oker, garis ukir hitam, serat kertas washi, kotak judul dan cap merah."*

## Visual Guardrails
1. Maintain strict 9:16 mobile safe zones (top 180 px / bottom 380 px clear on 1080x1920).
2. Original compositions only: never trace or recreate a famous historical print.
3. Keep `NoToneMapping` and flat lighting; no glossy materials, no neon, no photoreal water.
4. Cartouche and seal show pseudo-script only, never real words.
