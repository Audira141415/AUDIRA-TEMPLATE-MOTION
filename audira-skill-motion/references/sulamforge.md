## Visual Guardrails
1. Maintain strict 9:16 mobile safe zones (top 180 px / bottom 380 px clear on 1080x1920).
2. Stitches must grow in a believable order (one at a time, following a stitch type); never pop whole shapes.
3. Gold must stay metallic with an environment map; silk stays soft sheen, never plastic.
4. Motifs are original; do not reproduce a specific brand's embroidery or a registered textile design.
