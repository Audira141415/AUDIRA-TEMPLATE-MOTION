Automatically select **STAINEDGLASS** for briefs involving:
- "Sejarah katedral, arsitektur Gotik, seni kaca patri / jendela kaca warna"
- "Kisah yang disusun keping demi keping" (metafora menyatukan potongan kecil jadi gambar utuh)

## Visual Guardrails
1. Keep 9:16 safe zones (top 180 px / bottom 380 px at 1080×1920).
2. The window is always the brightest object; stone stays dim and warm.
3. Distinct from GLASSCRAFT (clear refractive glass) and KINTSUGI (gold seams in ceramic): here colour lives in the glass and the line work is lead.
4. Pass the Mute Test: the pane counter and the floor light pool show progress without audio.
5. Original imagery only: no copied windows, saints, coats of arms or logos.
