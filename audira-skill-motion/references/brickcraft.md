# Style 19: BRICKCRAFT (Tactile Plastic Bricks & Stop-Motion Macro)

> Stop-motion toy-brick films shot like macro photography on a real desk: a world built from interlocking plastic bricks with round studs, animated a pose at a time.

## 1. Visual Language
- **Materials:** High-gloss ABS plastic with clearcoat (0.85), micro-scratch roughness, contact occlusion darkening between stacked bricks, and matte baseplates.
- **Stud Pitch:** 1.0 unit pitch, cylindrical studs (radius 0.24, height 0.18) on top faces.
- **Environment:** Real wooden tabletop desk with subtle out-of-focus background items (coffee mug, notepad stack).
- **Lighting:** Studio softboxes casting rectangular specular highlights on plastic surfaces, warm directional key with sharp contact shadows, and cool rim kickers.

## 2. Motion Grammar
- **Stepping (15 FPS):** Character limb swings and flying brick placement step discretely on twos/fours.
- **Camera (60 FPS):** Macro crane glides, close-up toy tracking, and tabletop orbits remain silky smooth at 60 FPS.
- **Assembly Snaps:** Pieces overshoot slightly upon impact with physical click settling.

## 3. Best Applications
- Creative engineering and product assembly explainers.
- Playful startup growth and team-building narratives.
- Educational architectural models and whimsical diorama stories.
