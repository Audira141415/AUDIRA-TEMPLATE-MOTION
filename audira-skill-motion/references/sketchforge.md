## Distinct from

DRAFTFORGE is blueprint CAD with dimension lines on blue; VINTAGEFORGE is sepia copperplate manuscript; CHALKCRAFT is chalk on blackboard; BUILDFORGE assembles components. SKETCHFORGE is a single object's design-process transformation: graphite on a cream sketchbook → wire → clay → coloured model leaving the page.

## Visual Guardrails
1. Keep text inside the 9:16 safe zone (top 180 px / bottom 380 px of 1920, scaled by height/1920).
