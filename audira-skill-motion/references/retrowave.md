## 1. Visual Identity
- **Primary Aesthetic:** Kisi neon horizon tanpa batas, matahari terbenam faset 3D, scanline monitor tabung CRT, dan palet synthwave 80s.
- **Color Tokens:**
  - `voidDark`: `#0C081A`
  - `neonMagenta`: `#FF007F`
  - `synthCyan`: `#00F6FF`
  - `sunsetAmber`: `#FF9E00`
  - `phosphorGreen`: `#39FF14`
  - `chromeSilver`: `#E2E8F0`

## 2. Best Practices
1. **Determinism:** Never read `Date.now()` or call `Math.random()` in render loop. Always use `f` frame parameter.
2. **Camera Pacing:** Move the camera along spatial axes to reveal components.
3. **Contrast:** Maintain readable text and overlay within standard safe zones (Top 180px, Bottom 380px).
