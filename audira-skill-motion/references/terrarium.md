## Visual Guardrails
1. Keep the jar the hero: the full vessel must be readable in the hook before the macro push-in.
2. Biology claims (water cycle, photosynthesis, species) need sources in `research/SOURCE_REGISTER.md`; species shown are generic and stylised.
3. No fast cuts or shaky camera; this style sells calm.
4. Maintain 9:16 safe zones (top 180 px / bottom 380 px of 1080x1920 clear of text).
5. Mute Test: clock + day label + caption explain the phase without audio.
