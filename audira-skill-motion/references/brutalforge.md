## 1. Visual Identity
- **Primary Aesthetic:** Garis luar tebal hitam pekat, bayangan jatuh keras tanpa blur, warna pop neon elektrik, dan tipografi sans-serif ultra-bold.
- **Color Tokens:**
  - `neoYellow`: `#FFE600`
  - `cyberCyan`: `#00F0FF`
  - `acidGreen`: `#25F482`
  - `hotPink`: `#FF3864`
  - `inkBlack`: `#0B0C10`
  - `pureWhite`: `#FFFFFF`

## 2. Best Practices
1. **Determinism:** Never read `Date.now()` or call `Math.random()` in render loop. Always use `f` frame parameter.
2. **Camera Pacing:** Move the camera along spatial axes to reveal components.
3. **Contrast:** Maintain readable text and overlay within standard safe zones (Top 180px, Bottom 380px).
