## Rules

1. Every surface is textile: felt, wool, yarn, thread, wood or bead. No plastic gloss, no clay sheen.
2. Show the craft: stitches, threads and seams stay visible.
3. Puppets move on twos with tiny jitter; never smooth-interpolate a puppet.
4. Warm, cosy, soft: saturated but gentle colours, soft shadows.
5. Original characters only (no licensed plush characters or mascots).

## Known limits

- Fuzz shells are alpha-tested cutouts: very close macro shots show the speckle pattern; add shells or raise texture repeat for close-ups.
- Character limbs are simple primitives; complex gestures need extra joints.
- Wound-yarn geometry is heavy (long tubes); keep yarn balls at 6 or fewer per shot.
