# Style 20: URBANFORGE (Architectural White Model & Projected Data)

> A pure white massing model of a real urban district stands on a plinth in a dark exhibition room, and a projector paints data onto it as light: height metrics, zoning envelopes, and solar shadow studies.

## 1. Visual Language
- **Materials:** Matte architectural plaster/basswood (`#f5f5f2`, roughness 0.88), obsidian presentation plinth, dark satin water inlet cutouts.
- **Lighting:** Dark exhibition gallery ambient with a real directional sun light casting crisp shadow footprints, and overhead soft projector cone.
- **Projected Layers:** Vibrant luminous washes (Cyan for height/density, Amber for hero towers, Emerald for civic zoning) illuminating facades.

## 2. Motion Grammar
- **Solar Sweep:** Sun trajectory smoothly animates from morning to late afternoon, creating shifting shadow envelopes across streets.
- **Data Wavefront:** Animated projection sweep moves across the model, transforming white plaster buildings into data-active nodes.
- **Camera Kinematics:** Rooftop macro skimming $\rightarrow$ vertical crane pullback $\rightarrow$ high isometric 1:1000 masterplan orbit.

## 3. Best Applications
- Smart city and urban planning masterplans.
- Real estate development pitches and transit-oriented zoning.
- Environmental solar access and shadow impact studies.
- Civic infrastructure and flood mitigation models.
