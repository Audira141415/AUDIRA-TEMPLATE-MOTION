## Visual Guardrails
1. Maintain strict 9:16 mobile safe zones (top 180 px / bottom 380 px clear on 1080x1920).
2. Keep a single dominant warm light (blencong); the shadow side must stay amber, never neutral white.
3. Original puppet designs only; do not trace named traditional figures or any published puppet set.
4. Respectful tone: wayang is living heritage; no parody of the dalang or sacred elements.
