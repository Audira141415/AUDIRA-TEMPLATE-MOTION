## 1. Visual Identity
- **Primary Aesthetic:** Tipografi arsitektur 3D raksasa monolitik tempat kamera melesat menembus rongga huruf dengan pencahayaan volumetrik.
- **Color Tokens:**
  - `abyssBlack`: `#08080A`
  - `monolithTitanium`: `#2C2F36`
  - `volumetricLight`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `coreAccentGold`: `#D4AF37`
  - `horizonMist`: `#1A1D24`

## 2. Best Practices
1. **Determinism:** Never read `Date.now()` or call `Math.random()` in render loop. Always use `f` frame parameter.
2. **Camera Pacing:** Move the camera along spatial axes to reveal components.
3. **Contrast:** Maintain readable text and overlay within standard safe zones (Top 180px, Bottom 380px).
