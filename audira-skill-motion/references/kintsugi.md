## 1. Visual Identity
- **Primary Aesthetic:** Keramik porselen matte retak organik yang dialiri lelehan emas cair bercahaya, merefleksikan filosofi dan kemewahan.
- **Color Tokens:**
  - `porcelainMatte`: `#F7F7F9`
  - `obsidianCeramic`: `#121214`
  - `pureGoldLeaf`: `#E5A93C`
  - `goldEmissive`: `#FFD56B`
  - `tatamiBeige`: `#EFE9DC`
  - `shadowWarm`: `#302B27`

## 2. Best Practices
1. **Determinism:** Never read `Date.now()` or call `Math.random()` in render loop. Always use `f` frame parameter.
2. **Camera Pacing:** Move the camera along spatial axes to reveal components.
3. **Contrast:** Maintain readable text and overlay within standard safe zones (Top 180px, Bottom 380px).
