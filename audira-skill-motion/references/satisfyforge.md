## Rules

1. Precision is the story: nothing may miss, clip, or land crooked.
2. Pastel only: no black, no saturated primaries, no hard shadows.
3. One action at a time, readable at a glance; repeat the action rhythm (equal spacing).
4. The camera always moves, slowly; no cuts inside a loop.
5. No branded toys, slime brands or product logos.

## Known limits

- No real fluid or granular simulation: sand slices are rigid boxes and liquid is a clipped volume.
- Clipping planes are world-space; moving the mould requires moving the plane constants too.
- VSM shadows can bleed on very thin geometry; keep blades thicker than 0.01 units.
