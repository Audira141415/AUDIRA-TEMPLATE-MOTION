## Visual Guardrails
1. Keep 9:16 safe zones (top 180 px / bottom 380 px at 1080×1920).
2. Contrast check first: if the still looks milky, lower exposure/fog before touching colours.
3. Distinct from BACKROOMS (yellow office, fluorescent, VHS) and RETROWAVE (neon grid, synth sun).
4. Pass the Mute Test: the door opening and the stairs to nowhere tell the story without audio.
5. No characters, faces or brand objects; eeriness comes from architecture and stillness.
