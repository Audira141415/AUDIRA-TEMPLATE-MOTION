## Signature Motion & Style Rules

1. **Cold-Press Rag Paper Canvas:** Warm cream matte paper base (`#FAF6EF`, roughness 0.96).
2. **Layered Pigment Washes:** Translucent watercolor flats (opacity 0.78–0.88) simulating organic wet-on-wet absorption.
3. **Pencil Contour Details:** Delicate graphite pencil sketch lines defining characters and foliage edges.
4. **Gentle Floating Camera:** Meditative, floating macro drift recreating the intimate feel of turning an illustrated book page.
