# GROWTH ENGINE: The 50+ Visual & Auditory Hook Database
# AUDIRA RETENTION INTELLIGENCE

A video's retention curve is decided in the **first 1.8 seconds**. If the viewer does not see dynamic motion, intriguing contrast, or an emotional stake within 100 frames, they scroll away.

---

## 1. Top 10 High-Conversion Hook Archetypes

### Hook Archetype 1: The Abrupt Drop-In ("The Heavy Object")
- **Visual:** A 3D object drops vertically into frame with extreme acceleration and slams onto a surface with strong spring overshoot (`damping: 12`).
- **Sound:** Heavy wooden or metallic thud + sub-bass drop at frame 15.
- **Copy Trigger:** *"JANGAN SAMPAI SALAH PILIH..."* or *"99% DEVELOPER TIDAK TAHU INI..."*

### Hook Archetype 2: The Scale Illusion ("The Miniature Reveal")
- **Visual:** Opens tight on what appears to be a gigantic architectural building or machine. At 1.2s, a stylized toy hand reaches in and picks it up, revealing it is a tabletop miniature diorama.
- **Sound:** Deep cinematic rumble suddenly cutting to delicate paper rustle.
- **Copy Trigger:** *"KITA KIRA INI RUMIT... TERNYATA SE-SIMPLE INI."*

### Hook Archetype 3: The Exploded Teardown Snap
- **Visual:** A closed device immediately fractures into 25 floating internal components with a bright neon highlight flash.
- **Sound:** High-velocity hydraulic release + whoosh sweep.
- **Copy Trigger:** *"BONGKAR RAHASIA DI DALAMNYA..."*

### Hook Archetype 4: The Code-To-Life Leap
- **Visual:** Monospaced terminal typing at blazing speed, then a 3D character or golden coin physically leaps through the screen glass toward the viewer.
- **Sound:** Rapid keyboard clatter followed by glass shattering/portal whoosh.
- **Copy Trigger:** *"DARI KODE JADI CUAN..."*

### Hook Archetype 5: The Contrast Flash (Noir to Neon)
- **Visual:** Opens in moody 1940s black and white chiaroscuro; at 1.5s, an electric cyan laser wipes across the screen, converting the scene into full cyberpunk neon.
- **Sound:** Muffled vintage radio audio suddenly snapping into crisp modern stereo bass.
- **Copy Trigger:** *"DULU VS SEKARANG..."*
