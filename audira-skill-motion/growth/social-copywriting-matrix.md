# GROWTH ENGINE: 5-Platform Social Copywriting Matrix
# AUDIRA RETENTION INTELLIGENCE

A video release is incomplete without ready-to-publish copywriting tailored to the algorithmic patterns of the 5 major social video platforms.

---

## 1. The 5-Platform Output Protocol

Every completed video render automatically compiles optimized metadata across 5 formats:

### 1.1 TikTok (Short, Punchy, Curiosity-Driven)
- **Format:**
  ```text
  [Viral Hook Question] 🤯
  
  [1-2 Sentence Intrigue: What happened & Why it matters]
  
  Comment [KEYWORD] buat dapet [FREE RESOURCE / LINK]! 👇
  
  #AUDIRA #[StyleTopic] #TechTok #Animasi3D #Trending #[NicheTag]
  ```
- **Pin Comment:** Question triggering debate in comments to boost algorithmic interaction score.

### 1.2 Instagram Reels (Value-Packed & Aesthetic)
- **Format:**
  ```text
  [Bold Visual Headline] ✨
  
  [3 to 5 Bullet Point Value Breakdown]:
  • Poin 1: [Detail teknis menarik]
  • Poin 2: [Rahasia di balik animasi]
  • Poin 3: [Hasil nyata / insight]
  
  Simpan video ini buat referensi kamu nanti! 📌
  Follow @[Account] untuk update animasi 3D & AI setiap hari.
  
  Audio: [Original Audio / Trending Sound Name]
  #MotionDesign #3DAnimation #CreativeCoding #ReelsIndonesia #CGI
  ```

### 1.3 YouTube Shorts (SEO-Optimized & Evergreen)
- **Format:**
  - **Title:** Under 70 characters with primary keyword + bracket hook (e.g. `Bongkar Cara Kerja Mesin Jet 3D [Gak Nyangka!]`).
  - **Description:** 200 words containing high-search-volume keywords, chapters timestamp, and link to long-form tutorial or website.
  - **Tags:** 15 comma-separated tags under 500 characters.

### 1.4 Facebook Reels & Video (Storytelling & Discussion)
- **Format:**
  ```text
  Banyak yang belum tahu bagaimana proses di balik [TOPIK]...
  
  [Conversational Narrative Paragraph]: Ceritakan kisah dengan gaya bertutur hangat dan mudah dipahami semua kalangan usia.
  
  Menurut Anda, apakah teknologi seperti ini akan mengubah masa depan kita dalam 5 tahun ke depan? Tulis pendapat Anda di kolom komentar!
  ```

### 1.5 X / Twitter (Thread Starter & High-Data Punch)
- **Format:**
  ```text
  Kami baru saja membuat simulasi 3D [TOPIK] menggunakan AI motion intelligence.

  Ini 3 fakta gila yang kami temukan:
  
  1/ [Fakta Kunci + Angka Spesifik]
  2/ [Insight Mekanika/Desain]
  3/ [Kesimpulan Strategis]
  
  Tonton video lengkapnya di bawah 🧵👇
  ```
