## 2. Chronological Shot Breakdown

- **Era 1 (0.0s – 9.0s) — The Antique Beginning (e.g. Year 1920):**
  - *Materials:* Sepia parchment, cast iron, oiled mahogany wood.
  - *Lighting:* 2400K flickering amber candlelight, deep vignette shadows.
  - *Sound:* Gramophone vinyl crackle, ticking pocket watch.
  - *Date Overlay:* Giant embossed numbers: **"1924"**.

- **Era 2 (9.0s – 18.0s) — The Industrial Mid-Century (e.g. Year 1960):**
  - *Morph Transition:* Antique wood texture peels away as chrome trim and ivory bakelite plastic expand onto the mesh.
  - *Lighting:* Clean daylight fluorescent 4500K.
  - *Sound:* Rotary telephone dial, mechanical typewriter bell.
  - *Date Overlay:* Dynamic odometer counter rolling: **"1968"**.

- **Era 3 (18.0s – 27.0s) — The Early Digital Revolution (e.g. Year 1995):**
  - *Morph Transition:* Beige matte plastic, chunky CRT monitors, green phosphor text.
  - *Lighting:* Cool monitor blue spill, scanline raster.
  - *Sound:* 56k dial-up modem handshake, clicky keyboard clatter.
  - *Date Overlay:* Retro pixelated font: **"1998"**.

- **Era 4 (27.0s – 36.0s) — The Modern Era (e.g. Year 2026):**
  - *Morph Transition:* Casing melts down into seamless anodized aerospace aluminum and curved sapphire glass.
  - *Lighting:* Studio white LED softbox, pristine edge reflections.
  - *Sound:* Glass capacitive tap, subtle haptic motor click.
  - *Date Overlay:* Ultra-thin clean typography: **"2026"**.

- **Era 5 (36.0s – 45.0s) — The Speculative Frontier & Outro (e.g. Year 2050):**
  - *Morph Transition:* Hardware dissolves into floating optical holographic light fields and neural interface rings.
  - *Sound:* Ethereal synth chord, high-resonance pulse.
  - *Outro Card:* Final philosophical quote + Brand closure.
