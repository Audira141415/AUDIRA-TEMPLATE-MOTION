## 2. Shot-by-Shot Execution Breakdown

### Beat 1: Solid Exterior Presentation (0.0s – 5.0s / Frames 0 – 300)
- **Visual Action:** The machine or hardware appears in pristine closed condition on a dark technical pedestal. Specular highlight travels across the casing.
- **Camera:** 45° isometric view or 360° slow rotational scan.
- **Typography:** Technical specification kicker: *"TURBOJET V8 DOHC ENGINE // ARCHITECTURE 2026"*.
- **Audio Cue:** Low mechanical hum, laser barcode scan chime.

### Beat 2: The Exploded Disassembly (5.0s – 14.0s / Frames 300 – 840)
- **Visual Action:** Bolts unscrew with rapid spin; outer casing splits into transparent ghosted panels; internal shafts, pistons, gears, or microchips slide outward along orthogonal axes ($X, Y, Z$) with calibrated hydraulic damping.
- **Camera:** Dynamic rack-focus tracking inward through the floating internal components.
- **Typography:** Dimensioned callout brackets snap onto key parts with metric labels (e.g. *"Titanium Turbine Rotor: 42,000 RPM"*).
- **Audio Cue:** Sequential mechanical clicks, whooshes, and metallic lock sounds.

### Beat 3: Operational Flow & Internal Telemetry (14.0s – 24.0s / Frames 840 – 1440)
- **Visual Action:** The disassembled parts start functioning while floating! Electric currents or fuel mixtures pulse through transparent glass conduits with glowing amber/cyan particle streams. Thermal heatmap indicates friction hotspots.
- **Camera:** Fluid macro flight weaving between moving gears and silicon dies.
- **Typography:** Educational takeaway subtitle: *"Mekanisme pendingin ganda menjaga suhu tetap di bawah 85°C."*
- **Audio Cue:** Rising electronic hum, steady mechanical rhythm, whooshing fluid flow.

### Beat 4: Instant Re-Snap & Master Outro (24.0s – 30.0s / Frames 1440 – 1800)
- **Visual Action:** All floating sub-assemblies reverse direction simultaneously and slam back together with immense magnetic precision. The machine returns to solid form with a single neon pulse along its seam lines.
- **Camera:** Fast backward dolly reveal to full wide framing.
- **Typography:** Authority branding badge: *"ENGINEERED BY AUDIRA LABS // DETAIL LENGKAP DI UTAS"*.
- **Audio Cue:** Heavy hydraulic clamp slam (`sfx/clamp-slam.wav`) followed by a resonant bass decay.
