# RECIPE: Problem - Agitation - Solution (15-Second Commercial Hook)
# AUDIRA STORYBOARD RECIPES

**Recipe ID:** `recipe-pas-15s`  
**Duration:** 15.0 seconds (900 frames @ 60 FPS / 450 frames @ 30 FPS)  
**Best Styles:** `3d-toy-diorama`, `neo-brutalism`, `devforge`, `brutalforge`, `claymation`  
**Objective:** Maximum scroll-stopping retention and high conversion for product, app, or service announcements.

---

## 1. Narrative Blueprint

```
┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬──────────────────────┬─────────────────┐
│ Beat 1: Hook    │ Beat 2: Agitate │ Beat 3: Solve/Payoff │ Beat 4: CTA     │
│ 0.0s – 2.5s     │ 2.5s – 6.5s     │ 6.5s – 12.0s         │ 12.0s – 15.0s   │
│ (Frames 0–150)  │ (Frames 150–390)│ (Frames 390–720)     │ (Frames 720–900)│
└─────────────────┴─────────────────┴──────────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. Shot-by-Shot Execution Breakdown

### Beat 1: The Scroll-Stopping Hook (0.0s – 2.5s / Frames 0 – 150)
- **Visual Action:** Hero object or character drops abruptly into the frame from the top with strong spring overshoot (`stiffness: 220, damping: 14`). Dust particles or shockwave rings expand upon floor impact.
- **Camera:** Static or snap-in punch (`+10% zoom` on impact frame).
- **Typography:** Bold high-contrast headline in upper safe-zone: *"MASIH BIKIN MANUAL?"*
- **Audio Cue:** Heavy physical thud (`sfx/impact-wooden-thud.wav`) + sub-bass drop at frame 15.
- **Directorial Rule:** No gradual fade-ins! The hero prop must be physically present and moving within the first 10 frames.

### Beat 2: Agitation / Problem Visualization (2.5s – 6.5s / Frames 150 – 390)
- **Visual Action:** Secondary props representing friction, errors, or complexity clutter the scene (e.g. tangled cables, flying warning icons, red exclamation stamps slamming down).
- **Character Gesture:** Character scratches head, shrugs, or shows comical distress (ears droop, eyes blink rapidly).
- **Typography:** Red annotation badge slides in: *"Buang waktu 4 jam tiap hari..."*
- **Audio Cue:** Rapid ticking clock, error buzzer sound, or vinyl scratch.

### Beat 3: The Hero Solution & Feature Demonstration (6.5s – 12.0s / Frames 390 – 720)
- **Visual Action:** The clutter is wiped away in a clean wave transition. The solution prop emerges or lights up with a glowing primary brand aura. The character smiles, gestures with open hands, or points proudly.
- **Camera:** Gentle macro push-in or 15° orbital glide to spotlight the hero product.
- **Lighting Shift:** Studio lighting transitions from dim agitated mood to bright, warm, high-key golden highlights.
- **Typography:** Clean green/cyan value metric badge: *"1-KLIK SELESAI OTOMATIS"*.
- **Audio Cue:** High-velocity ascending chime, sparkling whoosh, warm BGM swell.

### Beat 4: Punchy Resolution & Call-To-Action (12.0s – 15.0s / Frames 720 – 900)
- **Visual Action:** Outro card scales into center with brand badge, interactive tap arrow animation, and official URL/handle.
- **Character Gesture:** Friendly wave goodbye or wink.
- **Typography:** Direct imperative CTA: *"Coba Sekarang — Link di Bio"*.
- **Audio Cue:** Crisp UI click + clean acoustic guitar/synth outro note.
