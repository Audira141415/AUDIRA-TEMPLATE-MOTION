## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Low Glide → Overhead Rise** (Catmull-Rom through time keys).
- **Vertical FOV:** `52°` during the glide, `50°` overhead.
- **Start:** `[0.5, 0.6, 6.4]` looking at `[0, 0, 3.6]`, just above the grout, behind the settling wave.
- **End:** `[0, 12.6, 2.1]` looking at `[0, 0, -0.1]`: near-overhead, picture "up" toward the top of frame.
- **Roll:** `±0.02 rad` during the glide only; zero once overhead.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Skim (0–5 s):** tiles fall past the lens and clink into the bed in front of the camera.
- **Climb (5–9 s):** the camera lifts while the wave finishes the far edge.
- **Reveal (9–12 s):** slow overhead drift so the full image reads; never a static frame.
