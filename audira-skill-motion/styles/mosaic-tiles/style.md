---

## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** Thousands of small hand-cut tesserae in terracotta, lapis, ochre, cream and gold smalti falling and settling into a bold outlined image on a lime grout bed; contour-following rows (andamento), slight tilt per tile, glaze sheen under a raking warm light.
- **Atmosphere:** Patient, warm, archaeological, quietly triumphant.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Ancient history, heritage, "many small parts make a whole", reveals of a symbol or subject.
