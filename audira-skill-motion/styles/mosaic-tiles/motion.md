# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

## 1. Frame Rate & Cadence
- **Target Frame Rate:** `60 FPS` final, `30 FPS` preview; everything derives from `t = frame / fps`.
- **Rhythm:** A patient, rhythmic wave of tiny impacts.

---

## 2. Motion Choreography
1. **Drop:** height fraction $1 - u^2$ over the first 72 % of a 1.1 s flight from 2.2–4.8 units, with lateral drift that converges on the slot.
2. **Spin Decay:** spin $(1 - u)(2 + 6s)$ rad on all three axes, zero at landing.
3. **Hop:** one 6 % hop $0.06\sin(\pi u)(1-u)$ after landing, then rest.
4. **Settling Wave:** delay $-1.3 + \frac{4.5 - z}{9}\cdot 8.0 + 0.9\,j$ s (near edge first; some tiles already seated at frame 0 so the hook is never empty).
5. **Dust:** slow drift $0.04$ u/s with sine wander.

---

## 3. Story Beats (12 s example)
- 0–2 s Hook: lapis and gold tiles rain past a lens skimming the bed.
- 2–8 s Development: the wave runs ahead of the glide, outlines and the bird's body appear underfoot.
- 9–11 s Payoff: the camera rises overhead and the full sun-bird reads in one glance; counter at 6,700.
