## 1. Frame Rate & Cadence
- **Target Frame Rate:** `30 FPS` or `60 FPS` with gentle organic sway.
- **Rhythm:** Calming, slow-paced, lullaby cadence.

---

## 2. Motion Choreography
1. **Foliage & Wind Sway:**
   - Grass blades and tree canopies sway using harmonic compound sines:
     $$\theta(t) = 0.04 \sin(1.2 t) + 0.02 \sin(2.5 t)$$
2. **Character Floating Bounce:**
   - Gentle walking bounce with a soft paper flutter secondary reaction.
3. **Floating Seeds / Petals:**
   - Dandelion seeds drift across the screen with gentle Perlin noise curl vectors.
