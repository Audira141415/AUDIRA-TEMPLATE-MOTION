# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

---

## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** Classic European picture-book aesthetic (Beatrix Potter, Winnie the Pooh, Oliver Jeffers), rough 300gsm cold-press paper texture with visible cotton deckled edges, gentle translucent watercolor washes with visible pigment pooling (granulation/bleed), soft pencil sketch line work, tiered 2.5D pop-up diorama layers, and storybook serif typography.
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, nostalgic, innocent, calming, poetic, and heartwarming.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Bedtime stories, gentle life advice, family/childhood memories, nature appreciation, and whimsical adventures.
