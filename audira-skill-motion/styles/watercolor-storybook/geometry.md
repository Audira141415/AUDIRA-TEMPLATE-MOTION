# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

## 1. Primary Mesh Archetypes
- **Layered 2.5D Paper Flats:** Multi-tiered cutout planes with subtle physical thickness (`depth: 0.02 - 0.04`) and deckled rough edges.
- **Organic Rolling Hills:** Gentle sinusoidal curves along the horizontal horizon.
- **Whimsical Storybook Characters:** Soft rounded silhouette, teardrop torsos, stubby legs, expressive ears.
- **Botanical Elements:** Hand-drawn oak trees, swaying wildflowers, and fluffy watercolor clouds.

---

## 2. Pop-Up Book Staging
- Layer 1 (Foreground): Grass blades, wildflowers with slight out-of-focus blur ($Z = 1.2$).
- Layer 2 (Subject): Storybook character on a grassy mound ($Z = 0.0$).
- Layer 3 (Middle Ground): Wooden fence, cottage chimney with curly smoke ($Z = -1.5$).
- Layer 4 (Background): Distant rolling hills, pastel sky wash ($Z = -3.8$).
