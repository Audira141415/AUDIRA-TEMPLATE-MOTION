## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Gentle Parallax Storybook Rig**.
- **Focal Length:** `55mm` equivalent.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `30°`.
- **Position:** `[0, 0.4, 3.8]`.
- **Depth of Field:** Subtle, preserving paper texture detail across the middle ground.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Gentle Floating Drift:** Subtle sinusoidal sway along $X$ (`±0.06 units`) to reveal the layered 2.5D pop-up depth between foreground grass and background hills.
- **Storybook Page Turn Transition:** Camera sweeps softly to the side as page layers peel away.
