## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Macro Needle-Tracking Rig** (`getSulamCamera`).
- **Focal Length:** macro `~70mm` (FOV 30°) opening to `~55mm` (FOV 34–36°).
- **Hook Pose:** target = 70% toward the live needle tip on petal one, camera offset `[+0.3, 0.46, +0.62]`.
- **Mid Pose:** `[0.9, 1.5, 1.7]` looking at the flower centre.
- **Wide / End Pose:** `[-0.35, 3.1, 2.5]` then `[0.1, 2.6, 1.95]` over the full hoop.
- **Near plane:** `0.01` for macro.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Track the Needle (0–1.6 s):** the frame follows the tip as each satin stitch is laid.
- **Rise & Arc (1.6–6.8 s):** ease-in-out climb; the flower fills under the lens.
- **Reveal (6.4–9.2 s):** pull up to show the hoop, gold ring, spools and songket.
- **Glint Push (9–12 s):** slow push-in while the sweep light crosses the gold.
- **Micro drift:** ±3 cm x, ±1.5 cm y at all times; no static frames.
