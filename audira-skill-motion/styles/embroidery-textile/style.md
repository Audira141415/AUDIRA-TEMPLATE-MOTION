## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** Extreme macro of hand embroidery: silk floss satin stitches growing one by one on linen stretched in a beech hoop, a steel needle pulling the thread taut, a French-knot flower centre, gold couched outlines, all resting on a plum songket cloth whose gold supplementary-weft floats glint as light passes.
- **Atmosphere:** Patient, warm, intimate, luxurious craft.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Craft process, textile heritage, fashion and UMKM products, stories of patience and detail.
