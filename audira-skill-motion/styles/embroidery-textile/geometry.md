## 1. Primary Mesh Archetypes
- **Stitch:** unit 8-sided cylinder rotated onto X; instance scale `(length x grow, r x 0.75, r)`, r 4.5–7.5 mm (scene units).
- **Satin Petals:** 5 petals x 39 stitches across a 0.46-long axis, width profile $0.135\sin^{0.6}$, slanted ±0.02.
- **Fishbone Leaves:** 2 leaves x 21 steps x 2 slanted stitches.
- **Stem Stitch:** 26 overlapping segments along a sine curve.
- **French Knots:** 46 instanced icosahedrons (r 16 mm) in a 0.075 disc.
- **Gold Ring:** 22 diamonds (4 sides + connecting bar) at radius 0.8.
- **Hoop:** inner torus R 1.0 / tube 0.035, outer torus R 1.045 / tube 0.038 (flattened 1.5), brass clamp box + screw.
- **Linen:** circle r 1.0 at y 0.1. **Songket:** 10 x 10 plane, 80x80 segments, soft folds outside r 1.3.
- **Needle:** 0.42 shaft + cone tip + eye torus; **live thread:** TubeGeometry r 4.5 mm.

## 2. Depth Staging
- Layer 1 (Foreground): needle, live thread, spools, loose gold thread.
- Layer 2 (Subject): stitched flower, leaves, gold ring.
- Layer 3 (Middle Ground): linen and hoop rings.
- Layer 4 (Background): songket cloth fading into warm-dark fog (4.5–9 m).
- Layer 5 (HUD): gold kicker, serif title, stitch counter, plum/gold badge, quote card, credit.
