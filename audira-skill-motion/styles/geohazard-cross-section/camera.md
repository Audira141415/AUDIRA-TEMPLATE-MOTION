## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Cut-Face to Coast Travel** (Catmull-Rom through timed keys).
- **Focal Length:** `32mm` equivalent.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `40°`.
- **Opening Position:** `[1.6, -0.8, 7.6]` aimed at the locked fault on the cut face.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Fault Close-Up (0–2.6 s):** slow push on the loading fault; the hook is the glow building.
- **Rupture Pull-Back (2.6–5 s):** damped shake (≤0.05 units, decays in ~1 s) while pulling back to see rings and the sea lifting.
- **Overview Arc (5–8 s):** elevated 3/4 arc from the ocean side across to the land side, following the landward pulse.
- **Coast Reveal (8–12 s):** settle above the cut face looking at the beach, town, route and beacon.
