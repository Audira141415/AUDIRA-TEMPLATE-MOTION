## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** a museum-grade sliced earth block on a lab plinth: wavy sediment and granite strata, dark pillow-basalt slab diving under the continent, a warm convection-swirled mantle, a coast with sand, green hills and a white-walled, red-roofed town model.
- **Event Layer:** amber-to-red locked fault, white rupture flash, pale-cyan seismic rings, down-dip chevrons, a glacier-blue sea with foam on the crest and a cut-face water curtain.
- **Atmosphere:** calm science lab, documentary clarity, reassuring rather than alarming.
- **Narrative Vibe:** how hazards work, preparedness, school and public-safety education.
