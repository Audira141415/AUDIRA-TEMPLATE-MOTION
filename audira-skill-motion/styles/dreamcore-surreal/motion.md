# STYLE DNA: Dreamcore Surreal — Motion & Temporal Dynamics
# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

---

## 1. Frame Rate & Cadence
- **Target Frame Rate:** `60 FPS` final, `30 FPS` preview; everything derives from `t = frame / fps`.
- **Rhythm:** Suspended, slow, breath-held.

---

## 2. Motion Choreography
1. **Weightless Bob:** $0.65\sin(0.55t+s) + 0.35\sin(0.31t+2.3s)$ scaled 0.03–0.08 for doors and clouds.
2. **Hook Door:** leaf opens $0 \to 1.15$ rad (smooth 0.3–3.2 s); doorway glow rises with it.
3. **Clouds:** drift $\pm0.04$–$0.12$ u/s, breathe $\pm3\%$.
4. **Bubbles:** rise 0.05–0.15 u/s with sine sway, wrapping every 4.5 units.
5. **Water:** ripple normal map offset $(0.004t, 0.006t)$.
6. **Payoff Door:** top-of-stairs door opens to ~1.15 rad and glows ×2.2 (8.5–10.5 s).

---

## 3. Story Beats (12 s example)
- 0–3 s Hook: a door standing in the pool opens by itself; sky light spills on the water.
- 3–8.5 s Development: float down the tiled room under indoor clouds and through the arch.
- 8.5–11 s Payoff: stairs rise out of endless water; the door at the top lights up.
