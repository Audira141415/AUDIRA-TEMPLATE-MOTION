## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Floating Drift** (Catmull-Rom through time keys).
- **Vertical FOV:** `48°` widening to `56°` after the arch.
- **Start:** `[-0.7, 1.0, 8.9]` just above the pool water, looking at the hook door `[1.0, 1.0, 4.6]`.
- **End:** `[0.7, 2.4, -4.6]` outside the arch, looking up the stairs `[1.8, 3.1, -14.5]`.
- **Roll:** `±0.025 rad` slow sway, like being carried by water.

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Hover (0–3 s):** the door opens on its own; the camera barely moves.
- **Drift (3–8 s):** down the room under the indoor clouds toward the arch.
- **Cross & rise (8–12 s):** through the arch at ~8.4 s, tilt up to the stairs and the glowing door.
