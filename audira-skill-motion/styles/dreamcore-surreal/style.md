- **Visual Core:** A white-tiled pool room with water for a floor and giant clouds under the ceiling, free-standing doors opening onto sky, a great arch onto an endless shallow sea, stairs rising out of the water to nowhere, pastel pink-lilac-blue sky, soft haze and iridescent bubbles.
