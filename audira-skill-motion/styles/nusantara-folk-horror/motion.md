## 2. Motion Choreography
1. **Wind:** $w(t) = 0.6\sin(0.9t) + 0.25\sin(2.3t + 1.2)$, plus a gust $0.5\sin(9t)$ while the lamps gutter. Drives bamboo lean and root sway.
2. **Pelita Swing & Flicker:** pendulum $0.06\sin(1.4t + seed)$; brightness $0.82 + 0.12\,n(7t) + 0.06\,n(19t)$ (value noise).
3. **Fog Drift:** each sprite slides sideways 0.12–0.32 m/s and wraps inside ±7 m.
4. **Kunang-kunang:** 34 fireflies, slow Lissajous drift, blink $0.5 + 0.5\sin(2.4t + \phi)$.
5. **Presence Timeline:** fade in 5.6–8.6 s, hold, fade out 10.6–11.3 s.
6. **Payoff Gutter:** lamps x0.22 and moon surge 8.9–9.5 s, recover 10.7–11.2 s.
