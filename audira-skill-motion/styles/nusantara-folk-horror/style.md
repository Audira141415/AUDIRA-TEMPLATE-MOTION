## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** A kampung footpath at midnight under a full moon. Thick ground fog in drifting banks, swinging pelita oil lamps on bamboo poles, dense bamboo groves, a wooden rumah panggung with woven gedek walls and one lit window, an old banyan (beringin) whose aerial roots hang like a curtain. Somewhere in the fog a pale silhouette with long dark hair appears and vanishes.
- **Atmosphere:** Quiet dread, folklore, hushed and humid, suggestive rather than graphic.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Cerita rakyat, legenda kampung, "malam Jumat" storytelling, cautionary tales, mystery hooks.
- **Distinct from:** BACKROOMS (western fluorescent liminal) and FILMNOIR (urban chiaroscuro). MISTISFORGE is rural, organic, moonlit and fog-driven.
