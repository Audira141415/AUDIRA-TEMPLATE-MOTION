## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Creeping Dolly Rig** (`getMistisCamera`).
- **Focal Length:** `28mm` equivalent, tightening to about `32mm` at the payoff.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `52°` to `45°` (8.8–10.2 s).
- **Position:** `[0.25, 1.5, 8.6]` to about `[-0.1, 1.4, 3.6]`, eye height.

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Creeping Forward Dolly:** 4.6 m along the path over 12 s with cubic ease-in-out; the viewer feels pulled, not walking.
- **Handheld Breath:** value-noise offsets ±4 cm (x) and ±2.5 cm (y) at 0.6–0.7 Hz. Never jitter.
- **Gaze Drift:** the look-at target blends lamp to path (0.6–3.2 s), then toward the presence (5.8–9.4 s), then partly releases.
- **Involuntary Push-In:** +0.45 m forward between 9.0–9.6 s, synced with the lamps guttering.
- **Forbidden:** whip pans, orbits around the presence (it is a billboard), drone shots.
