## 1. Primary Mesh Archetypes
- **Bamboo Grove:** instanced 8-sided culms (r 0.035–0.07, h 6–10 m) with node-band texture, plus instanced alpha leaf sprays (5 per culm).
- **Banyan (Beringin):** 5 tapered trunk cylinders, 9 flat-shaded dodecahedron canopy lobes, about 110 instanced aerial roots hanging 1.2–4 m.
- **Rumah Panggung:** 3x3 timber stilts, plank floor, woven gedek wall box, steep triangular-prism thatch roof, lit window with shutter bars, ladder.
- **Pelita:** bamboo pole and arm, tin reservoir, open glass chimney, emissive flame, additive glow sprite, point light.
- **Ground:** packed-earth plane, translucent footpath strip, instanced grass tufts.
- **Presence:** billboard plane (pale gown, long dark hair, no face), dark aura sprite behind, fog veil at the feet.
