# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Steady Orbit + World Zoom**: the lens never zooms; the world is rescaled every frame.
- **Focal Length:** `38mm` equivalent.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `36°`.
- **Position:** orbit radius `10.4 → 9.6`, yaw `-0.24 → +0.22 rad` over 12 s, height `1.0 → 1.6 → 1.0`.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Continuous Orbit:** the slow yaw gives parallax on every subject so no frame is a still.
- **Decade Jumps:** exponent eases with smootherstep (8% linear blend) between subject keys: long hold, quick jump.
- **Focus Hand-off:** the focus point moves linearly in S (not log S), so the old subject stays centred until it is tiny.
