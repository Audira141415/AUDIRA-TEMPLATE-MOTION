## 1. Primary Mesh Archetypes
- **Ant (5 mm):** ellipsoid gaster, petiole, thorax and head, 6 two-segment legs, antennae; modelled in mm and scaled ×0.001.
- **Scale Figure (1.75 m):** capsule mannequin in architectural blue-grey.
- **Skyscraper (330 m):** three setback boxes + spire with a canvas curtain-wall facade.
- **Mountain (8.8 km):** 72x26 cone, seeded angular bumps and ridges, snowline vertex colours, flat shading.
- **Earth (12,742 km):** 96x64 sphere + cloud shell + additive atmosphere shell.
- **Sun (1.39 million km):** granulated photosphere sphere + additive corona sprite.
- **Ruler:** up to 640 instanced major and minor ticks, accent edge line, white or dark bar.

---

## 2. Staging Layers
- Layer 1 (Foreground): drifting motes (90 instanced).
- Layer 2 (Subject): the one subject in its hold window + bracket + label.
- Layer 3 (Middle Ground): ruler and the shrinking previous subject.
- Layer 4 (Background): studio floor / star field.
- Layer 5 (HUD): frame readout, log gauge, caption, credit.
