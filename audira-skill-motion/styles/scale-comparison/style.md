---

## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** a clean measuring studio (white cyclorama, soft contact shadows) with a precise ground ruler and orange measuring brackets; subjects are crisp product-CGI models; the studio dissolves into a star field as the exponent passes the kilometre range.
- **Measuring Layer:** re-decading ticks, `10ⁿ m` labels, a live `FRAME 10^x m` readout and a vertical log gauge.
- **Atmosphere:** wonder through precision: calm, clean, numerate.
- **Narrative Vibe:** how big / how small, powers of ten, perspective and humility.
