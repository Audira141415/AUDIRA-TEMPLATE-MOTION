# STYLE DNA: 3D Toy Diorama — Camera & Optical Framing
# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

---

## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Strictly Static Theatrical Macro Rig**.
- **Focal Length:** `50mm` equivalent.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `32°` (Perspective projection).
- **Camera Position:** `[0, 1.25, 4.6]` (Eye-level to slight high-angle looking into the diorama stage).
- **Target Point:** `[0, 0.75, 0]` (Centered directly on the hero character's chest).
- **Sensor Simulation:** Full Frame 35mm (`36mm x 24mm`).

---

## 2. Depth of Field (DOF) & Bokeh
- **Aperture / f-stop:** `f/2.8` (Macro lens shallow focus).
- **Focal Plane:** Pinned strictly to the character's facial plane.
- **Foreground Bokeh:** Mild optical blur on the nearest footlight rim (`blur: 4px`).
- **Background Bokeh:** Soft cinematic blur on the scenery mountain flats and distant backdrop.

---

## 3. Strict Camera Movement Guardrails
- **PROHIBITED:**
  - ❌ No camera pan, tilt, or roll.
  - ❌ No zoom in / zoom out during the performance.
  - ❌ No handheld organic shake or wandering drift.
- **WHY:** The charm of a toy diorama relies completely on the audience feeling like they are peering into a physical, real-world miniature shadowbox or puppet theater. Moving the camera breaks the illusion of scale.
