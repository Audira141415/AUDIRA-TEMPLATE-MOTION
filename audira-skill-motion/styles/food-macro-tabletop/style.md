## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** High-end food advertising shot on a macro lens: one hero dish on a dark oiled-wood tabletop, ingredients suspended and falling in slow motion, steam backlit by a hot rim light, glossy sauce drips, appetising saturated warm colours, shallow depth-of-field bokeh.
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, hungry, intimate, premium, late-night craving.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Recipes, menu launches, culinary explainers, ingredient stories, food reviews.
