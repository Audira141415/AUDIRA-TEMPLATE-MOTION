## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** A designer's sketchbook page under a warm desk lamp: graphite construction lines, sketchy double strokes and hatching become a real object in four readable passes (wire, clay, colour, lift), limited to graphite, paper cream and one accent.
- **Atmosphere:** Quiet, crafted, satisfying, focused — a maker's desk at night.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Design process reveals, idea-to-product stories, architecture concepts, education in drawing and 3D modelling.
