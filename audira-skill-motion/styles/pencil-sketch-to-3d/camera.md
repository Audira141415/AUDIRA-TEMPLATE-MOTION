## 1. Camera Specifications
- **Rig Type:** Top-down → Orbit → Hero.
- **Vertical FOV:** `36°`, tightening to `32°` mid-orbit.
- **Distance:** ~10 units while drawing (the full sketch fits the 9:16 width), ~9 units at the hero angle.

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Draw:** above-the-shoulder view, slow descent `(0.9,10.4,5.4) → (0.6,9.3,4.7)`.
- **Extrude/Clay:** ease-in-out orbit to `(5.6, 4.3, 7.4)` looking at the house centre.
- **Payoff:** continue around to a low `(−4.3, 2.3, 7.4)` hero angle; target follows the lift.
