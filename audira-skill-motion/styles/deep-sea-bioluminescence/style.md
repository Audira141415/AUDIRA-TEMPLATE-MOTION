## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** A vertical descent through the water column: teal god-rays fading above, near-black blue water, drifting marine snow, translucent jellyfish pulsing cyan / magenta / violet light, and a predator revealed only by its own lure. A submersible sonar HUD and depth meter frame the dive.
- **Atmosphere:** Awe, hush, pressure, curiosity turning into quiet dread.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Ocean science, the unknown, "what lives where no light reaches", metaphors of going deeper.
