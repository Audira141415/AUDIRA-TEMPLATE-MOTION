## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Descending Helix Submersible Rig**.
- **Focal Length:** `32mm` equivalent, easing to `40mm` for the payoff.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `46°` → `38°`.

- **Roll:** `±0.03 rad` buoyancy roll.
