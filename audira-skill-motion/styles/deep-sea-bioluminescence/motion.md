## 2. Motion Choreography
1. **Jet Pulse:** $c(t) = \max(0, \sin(\omega t + \phi))^{2.4}$; bell scale $(1-0.2c,\ 1+0.14c,\ 1-0.2c)$; slow rise $0.12\,t$.
2. **Tentacle Wave:** travelling sine $0.18\,u\,\sin(1.6t - 5u + k)$ along each tentacle.
3. **Marine Snow:** fall $0.04$–$0.12$ u/s with $\pm0.15$ lateral drift.
4. **Plankton Twinkle:** scale $0.35 + 0.65\,(0.5+0.5\sin(ft+\phi))^3$.
5. **Lure Ignition:** stepped flicker 8.6–9.4 s, then $1 + 0.08\sin(5.3t)$; jaw opens 9.2–10.6 s.
