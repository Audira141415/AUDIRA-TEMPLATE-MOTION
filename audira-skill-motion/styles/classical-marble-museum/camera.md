## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Reverent Orbit** around the head (`[0, 2.58, 0]`).
- **Vertical FOV:** `30°` (≈ 70 mm portrait lens) widening to `36°`.
- **Orbit:** angle `+0.38 → −0.62 rad` with cubic ease-in-out over 12 s; radius `1.75 → 4.3` (smooth 0.6–8.5 s); height `2.63 → 2.45`; target drops from the eyes to the chest (`y 2.6 → 2.2`).
