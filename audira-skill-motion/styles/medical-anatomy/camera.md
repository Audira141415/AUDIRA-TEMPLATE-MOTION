## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Clinical Orbit** (continuous yaw `-0.55 + 0.13·t` rad).
- **Focal Length:** `42mm` equivalent.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `34°`.
- **Radius:** `6.3 → 7.6` (pull back during the reveal) `→ 7.0` (payoff push-in); height `0.95 → 0.4 → 0.65`.

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Hook (0–2 s):** closer, slightly high, on the opaque beating organ.
- **Reveal (2–5 s):** pull back while the wall fades so the whole circuit fits the frame.
- **Explain (5–9 s):** steady orbit; labels appear one by one and their leader lines track the anatomy via `projectToScreen`.
- **Payoff (9–12 s):** gentle push-in as rings flare; the orbit never stops.
