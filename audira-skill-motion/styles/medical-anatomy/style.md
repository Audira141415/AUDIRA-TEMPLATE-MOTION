## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** a glossy, softly sheened organ floating above a hologram projector in a deep-teal imaging suite; inside, saturated red and blue chambers and translucent glass vessels carry glowing blood cells.
- **Instrument Layer:** thin teal rings orbiting at different tilts, a scanning slice ring sweeping up the organ, floor rings pulsing with each beat, an ECG strip and heart-rate readout.
- **Atmosphere:** calm, precise, reassuring, hospital-grade.
- **Narrative Vibe:** how the body works, health literacy, patient education, school biology.
