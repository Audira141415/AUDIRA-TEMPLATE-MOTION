## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** An Edo-style woodblock print pulled apart into layered washi sheets. A towering wave rising from the right with claw-like foam fingers, rows of carved waves, long rowing boats, a twin-peak mountain with a dripping snow cap, ochre cloud ribbons, a vermilion sun in a bokashi sky, a cream cartouche with vermilion border and a red seal.
- **Atmosphere:** Elegant, graphic, timeless, powerful nature rendered with restraint.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Sea and nature stories, Japanese history and culture, art-technique explainers, poster-like hooks.
