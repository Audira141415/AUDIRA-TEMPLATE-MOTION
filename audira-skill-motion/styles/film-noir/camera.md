## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Moody Cinematic Tracking & Dutch Angle Rig**.
- **Focal Length:** `40mm` anamorphic equivalent.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `38°`.
- **Dutch Angle / Roll:** Permissible subtle tilt ($-3.5^\circ$ to $+4.0^\circ$) to heighten psychological disorientation and suspense.
- **Aperture / f-stop:** `f/2.0` (Shallow focal plane with anamorphic oval bokeh).

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Slow Low-Angle Track-In:** Camera slides forward at `0.02 units/frame`, rising gently from ankle level to chest level.
- **Venetian Blind Shadow Sweep:** The shadows drift across the frame slowly as if caused by a car passing on the cross street.
- **No Fast Action Whip-Cuts:** Cuts are deliberate, slow, and tension-building.
