# STYLE DNA: Film Noir — Geometry & Mesh Topology
# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

---

## 1. Primary Mesh Archetypes
- **Alley Architecture:** High vertical brick walls flanking narrow alley, fire escape metal stairs with zigzag railings, telephone pole wires cutting across the sky.
- **Venetian Blinds Gobo:** Parallel slitted rectangular blind bars that project crisp slatted shadow lines across the scene.
- **Detective Silhouette:** Fedora brim angle tilted at $15^\circ$, wide-lapel trench coat with raised collar, hands in pockets.
- **Puddle Geometry:** Irregular low-lying planes slightly indented into the asphalt road surface.

---

## 2. Poly Budget & Silhouette Rules
- **Poly Budget:** Mid-to-High Poly (`45,000 - 85,000 triangles`) to support detailed architectural brickwork and fire escape steel grating.
- **Silhouette Sharpness:** Hard, defined contours; no rounded toy bevels. Edges must produce razor-sharp shadow boundaries under direct tungsten key lights.
