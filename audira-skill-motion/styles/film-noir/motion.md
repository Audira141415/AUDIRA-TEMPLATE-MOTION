# STYLE DNA: Film Noir — Motion & Temporal Dynamics
# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

---

## 1. Frame Rate & Cadence
- **Target Frame Rate:** `24 FPS` cinematic feel (or `60 FPS` with motion-blur vector interpolation).
- **Temporal Rhythm:** Slow, deliberate, suspenseful pacing.

---

## 2. Motion Choreography
1. **Rainfall Physics:**
   - Rain streaks fall vertically with slight wind drift ($X: -0.05$).
   - Puddle ripples expand from impact points with decaying sinusoidal concentric rings.
2. **Smoke / Fog Drift:**
   - Cigarette smoke or steam from street manhole covers drifts upward in curled laminar flow.
3. **Detective Turn:**
   - Head turns deliberately toward the camera over 48 frames (2.0s).
   - Trench coat hem flutters gently in night breeze.
