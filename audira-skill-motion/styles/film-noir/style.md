## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** Classic 1940s post-war detective cinema, extreme chiaroscuro contrast, hard venetian blind shadows cast against damp brick walls, wet asphalt reflections, trench-coated silhouette, foggy atmospheric haze, glowing neon cocktail sign in monochrome, and vintage serif typography.
- **Atmosphere:** Moody, mysterious, tense, suspenseful, rain-soaked, and melancholic.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Gritty urban investigations, deep secrets, nocturnal crime thrillers, and philosophical monologue.
