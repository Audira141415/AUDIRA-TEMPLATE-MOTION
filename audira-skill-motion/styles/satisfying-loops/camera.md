# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Three-Quarter Studio Dolly** (`getSatisfyforgeCamera`).
- **Focal Length:** `70mm` equivalent (vertical FOV `36°`), low distortion for clean geometry.
- **Position:** `[stationX, 3.5, 5.6]` looking at `[stationX, 0.45, 0]` (~32° down).
- **Depth of Field:** none needed; clarity is part of the satisfaction.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Station Glide:** lateral move between plinths on cubic in-out (`stationX`: 3.5–4.5 s and 7.4–8.4 s).
- **Constant Sway:** `±0.35` units sinusoidal x drift so the frame never freezes.
- **Payoff Drop:** 9.4–11.6 s the camera rises and closes in (`height 4.6`, `dist 3.7`) for a near top-down view of the filled board.
