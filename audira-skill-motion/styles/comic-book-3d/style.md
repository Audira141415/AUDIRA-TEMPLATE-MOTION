## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** Golden/Silver-age inspired Western comic printed in 3D: flat cel bands, thick black inverted-hull ink lines, Ben-Day dots in the shadow band, CMYK process colours on newsprint, printed page panels with gutters and caption boxes, speech balloons and onomatopoeia bursts.
- **Atmosphere:** Loud, heroic, kinetic, optimistic — a Saturday-morning comic come alive.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Hero origin moments, city-saving action, product 'superpowers', energetic explainers, sports highlights.
