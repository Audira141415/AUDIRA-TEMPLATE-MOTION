## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** A premium coach's tactics board rendered as a physical miniature: mowed grass stripes, crisp painted chalk lines, glossy acrylic puck tokens with numbers, brushed aluminium board lip, small white goals with real nets, sitting inside a dark stadium bowl of out-of-focus crowd lights and floodlights.
- **Telestrator Layer:** yellow passing lanes that draw themselves on, dashed coral run arrows with heads, a ball trail, gold goal pulses and a gold replay path of the whole move.
- **Atmosphere:** Match-night broadcast studio: analytical, confident, kinetic.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Tactical breakdowns, goal replays, formation explainers, coaching lessons.
