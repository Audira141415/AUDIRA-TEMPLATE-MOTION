## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Broadcast Analysis Sweep** (Catmull-Rom spline through timed keys, never stops).
- **Focal Length:** `28–35mm` equivalent.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `44°` overhead → `40°` pitch-side.
- **Opening Position:** `[0.4, 12.6, 6.2]` looking at the centre circle (whole 4-3-3 shape readable in 9:16).
- **Closing Position:** low pitch-side at goal height `y ≈ 1.0–2.3`, target the goal mouth `[0, 0.2, -4.6]`.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Tactical Overhead (0–3 s):** slow drift down the pitch; formation is the subject.
- **Flank Sweep (3–6 s):** camera banks over the flank where the overload happens, following the ball side.
- **Low Broadcast Angle (6–9 s):** drops to goal height for the cross and the finish; tokens in the foreground give scale.
- **Replay Orbit (9–12 s):** slow lateral arc around the scorer while the gold replay path draws.
