## 1. Primary Mesh Archetypes
- **Cabinet:** one `ExtrudeGeometry` of the side profile (material groups: side art on caps, cobalt on walls).
- **CRT:** 24×24 plane bulged by `0.035·(1−x²)(1−y²)`.
- **Room:** instanced cabinet bodies, screens, marquees and halos.
- **World:** instanced voxel terrain (3 deep), sprite voxels (2 deep), 8-sided coin cylinders, star bitmap cubes.
