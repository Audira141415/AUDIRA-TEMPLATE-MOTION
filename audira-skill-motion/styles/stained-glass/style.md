## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** A tall pointed lancet window crowned by a rose, made of hundreds of jewel-tone panes held in dark lead cames, assembling piece by piece; sunlight pours through and casts coloured light pools onto a stone floor where dust motes drift in the shafts.
- **Atmosphere:** Reverent, warm, quiet, luminous; a moment of revelation.
- **Narrative Vibe:** History, craft, legends and fables, "small pieces making a whole", hope and first light.
