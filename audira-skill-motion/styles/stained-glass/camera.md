# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Reverent Pull-Back Rig** (Catmull-Rom through time keys, never stops).
- **Focal Length:** `38°` vertical FOV at the hook (≈ 35 mm) widening to `60°` for the final framing.
- **Start:** `[0.25, 2.7, 3.1]` looking at the rose heart `[0, 2.75, 0]`.
- **End:** `[-1.0, -2.0, 12.4]` looking at `[0, -1.1, 1.0]`: floor light pool in the lower third, window above.
- **Roll:** `±0.012 rad`, barely perceptible.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Nose to glass (0–2 s):** the first panes fly past the lens and seat in the rose.
- **Pull back (2–8 s):** reveal the lancet assembling top-down, columns slide past in the foreground.
- **Sink and settle (8–12 s):** camera lowers so the light on the floor leads the eye up to the sunlit rose.
