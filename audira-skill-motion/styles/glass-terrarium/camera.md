# STYLE DNA: Glass Terrarium — Camera & Optical Framing
# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

---

## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Macro Orbit, Descending** (yaw `0.45 + 0.2·t` rad, ~140° in 12 s).
- **Focal Length:** `42mm` equivalent.
- **Vertical Field of View (FOV):** `34°`.
- **Radius / Height:** `6.4 → 3.5` and `2.7 → 0.95`; target drifts from the jar centre to the snail.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Hook (0–2 s):** whole jar, slightly high, sun glinting on the glass.
- **Circle (2–8 s):** steady orbit while descending; refraction shifts as the glass curves past the lens.
- **Push-in (8–12 s):** eye level with the moss and the snail as night falls.
- **Foreground:** a blurred fern frond is locked to the lens' lower-left (`leafPose`), so parallax never stops.
