---

## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** a hand-blown glass jar with a cork stopper on a walnut table; through the curved glass you see gravel, a charcoal line, dark soil, velvet moss, feathery ferns, pebbles around a tiny pool and pale/orange mushrooms.
- **Life Layer:** droplets beading and sliding on the glass, ripples in the pool, ferns breathing, a snail gliding, fireflies and glowing caps at night.
- **Atmosphere:** cosy, meditative, wonder at small things.
- **Narrative Vibe:** closed ecosystems, the water cycle, plant care, slow living.
