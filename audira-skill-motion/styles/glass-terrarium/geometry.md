# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

## 1. Primary Mesh Archetypes
- **Jar:** `LatheGeometry` (15-point profile, 128 segments): flat base, straight wall, shoulder, neck, lip; cork cylinder stopper.
- **Substrate:** three cylinders (gravel 0.2, charcoal 0.07, soil to y 0.5) + 96x96 soil-top plane clipped to the jar radius and displaced by `soilY`.
- **Moss:** 1,300 instanced icosahedrons (r 0.022–0.052) in 16 seeded clusters.
- **Ferns:** 3 plants, 7–9 fronds, 20 leaflet pairs each (instanced flattened spheres along an arching frond curve).
- **Mushrooms:** 6 stems + half-sphere caps; **Pool:** disc + ripple rings; **Pebbles:** 28 instanced dodecahedrons.
- **Snail:** 46 instanced spheres on a log spiral (shell) + capsule foot + eye stalks.
- **Condensation:** 460 instanced flattened droplets on the inner wall.

## 2. Staging Layers
- Layer 1 (Foreground): out-of-focus fern frond, droplets.
- Layer 2 (Subject): moss, ferns, mushrooms, snail.
- Layer 3 (Middle Ground): glass jar and substrate bands.
- Layer 4 (Background): walnut table, sky dome, window bokeh.
- Layer 5 (HUD): clock + day arc, phase caption, credit.
