## 1. Studio Lighting Rig: `graphic_hard_sun`

### Key Light (Primary Illumination):
- **Color Temperature:** `5500K`
- **Intensity:** `2`
- **Orientation Angle:** `[45, 45, 0]`

### Fill Light (Shadow Softener):
- **Color Temperature:** `5500K`
- **Intensity:** `0.1`

### Rim / Kicker Light (Edge Separator):
- **Color Temperature:** `6500K`
- **Intensity:** `1`
- **Purpose:** Carves subject silhouette cleanly away from background void.
