## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** A candlelit writing desk. An open parchment codex rests on a slanted lectern: border vines in lapis, vermilion and verdigris, a lapis initial box carrying a raised gold-leaf ornament that gleams, and fresh iron-gall ink lines written one after another by a quill. Beside it, a Balinese-style lontar bundle: palm-leaf strips incised with rounded pseudo-glyphs, strung on a red cord between carved wooden covers, fanning open.
- **Atmosphere:** Quiet, reverent, warm, patient, knowledge being preserved.
- **Narrative Vibe:** History of writing and books, Nusantara manuscripts (lontar, babad), libraries and archives, literacy.
- **Distinct from:** VINTAGEFORGE (sepia copperplate engraving, Da Vinci sketches) and ISLAMICDOCUMENTARY (sacred geometry, domes).
