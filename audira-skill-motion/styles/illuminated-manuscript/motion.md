## 2. Motion Choreography
1. **Writing:** line $i$ starts at $1.9 + 0.55i$ s and takes 0.5 s; strip width and UV repeat = smoothstep progress. Line 7 is a red rubric.
2. **Quill:** nib rides the line end; tiny lift $0.004|\sin(38t)|$ while writing, then rises away after 8.6 s.
3. **Lontar Cover:** lifts and slides back, rotating -0.5 rad (5.6–6.6 s, ease-in-out).
4. **Leaf Fan:** leaf $k$ rotates $\mathrm{fan}\,(k - 0.15)\,1.6$ rad about the cord, spacing grows by 2.5 mm (6.2–9.2 s).
5. **Gold Gleam:** gleam light sweeps across the initial and emissive pulses with $\sin(\pi k)$ in the hook and the payoff.
6. **Dust Motes:** slow rise and sway in the candle light, twinkle $0.5 + 0.4\sin(1.7t + \phi)$.
