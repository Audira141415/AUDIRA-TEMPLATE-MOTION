## 1. Primary Mesh Archetypes
- **Desk & Wall:** walnut box 4.2 x 0.1 x 2.6, dark back wall plane at z -1.5.
- **Lectern:** 1.5 x 1.0 board tilted -0.5 rad about X at `[0, 0.55, -0.25]`, support slab, book-rest lip.
- **Codex:** leather binding box, two page blocks, two 0.62 x 0.86 page planes in a shallow V (±0.06 rad).
- **Gold Initial:** 0.19 plane with `alphaMap` ornament (abstract knot), 3 mm above the page.
- **Ink Lines:** 12 strips (0.48 x 0.032) on the right page, width and UV repeat grow together.
- **Quill:** feather plane 0.1 x 0.42 + nib cylinder, leaning out of the page.
- **Lontar:** 18 leaf boxes 0.8 x 0.004 x 0.066 (scaled 1.3), two carved covers, cord tube, coin.
- **Candles:** brass dish, wax cylinder with drips, flame ellipsoid, additive halo sprite.
