# STYLE DNA: Illuminated Manuscript & Lontar — Camera & Optical Framing
# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

---

## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Gold-Macro-to-Desk Rig** (`getManuscriptCamera`).
- **Focal Length:** `~55mm` (FOV 34–38°) in close shots, `~45mm` (FOV 42°) for the reveal.
- **Hook Pose:** 0.72 m along the page normal from the gold initial (`pageToWorld(-0.455, 0.265)`).
- **Writing Pose:** 1.15 m along the page normal, target 60% toward the pen point.
- **Lontar Pose:** `[0.75, 1.05, 1.65]` looking at `[0.98, 0.05, 0.42]`.
- **Reveal Pose:** `[0.2, 1.3, 2.55]` looking at `[0.08, 0.42, -0.15]`.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Gleam Hook (0–1.6 s):** hold on the initial while the gleam light slides across.
- **Follow the Pen (1.6–5.4 s):** ease to the right page; the target leans toward the nib as lines are written.
- **Track to the Lontar (5.4–7.2 s):** move down and right over the desk while the cover lifts.
- **Reveal (8.6–10.8 s):** pull back and up to the whole desk; slight push-in to 12 s.
- **Micro drift:** ±2 cm x, ±1.2 cm y always.
- **9:16 framing:** keep action inside the centre column; top 180 px and bottom 380 px stay clear.
