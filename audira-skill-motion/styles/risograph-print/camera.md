# AUDIRA STYLE DNA LIBRARY

## 1. Camera Specifications
- **Rig Type:** Top-down → Pop-up Dolly.
- **Vertical FOV:** `34°` → `40°` during the dolly.
- **Up Vector:** blends from `(0,0,-1)` (screen-up toward the spine while top-down) to world up between 1.6 s and 4.0 s, so the swing has no flip.

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Hook:** 10–11.5 units above the spread, slow descent.
- **Swing:** ease-in-out arc to the page front `(−0.25, 2.3, 14.2)` while layers pop.
- **Dolly:** low push to `(−0.2, 1.5, 9.8)` through grass and plant toward the sun.
- **Rise:** +0.45 units on the payoff; a 0.05-unit hand drift throughout.
