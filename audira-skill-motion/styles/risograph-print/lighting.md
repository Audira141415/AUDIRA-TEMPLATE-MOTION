## 1. Flat Paper Daylight
- `flat` canvas (no tone mapping) so cream stock and inks keep their printed values.
- Ambient `1.25` warm white + one key `1.35` from front-top-right casting soft PCF shadows of the standing layers onto the pages (this is the only 3D shading cue).
- Inks are unlit by design: real riso ink does not shade.
