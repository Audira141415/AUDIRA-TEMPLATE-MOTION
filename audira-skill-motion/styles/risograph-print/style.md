## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** Indie risograph zine: uncoated cream stock, three fluorescent/standard spot inks laid down one drum at a time, overprints creating secondary colours, coarse halftone screens, paper-tooth speckle, slight misregistration, staples and registration marks, layered cut-out shapes forming a city and plant.
- **Atmosphere:** Handmade, warm, playful, indie, a little imperfect on purpose.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Community events, indie shops, city and nature stories, music releases, soft explainers.
