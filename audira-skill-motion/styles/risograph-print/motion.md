## 2. Choreography
1. **Pop-up:** layer i lifts from 1.5 s + 0.42 s × i over 0.9 s with `easeOutBack(1.3)` — paper overshoots then settles.
2. **Unfurl:** leaves scale 0.45 → 1 and rotate open, staggered 0.12.
3. **Sunrise:** yellow disk eases from y 1.8 to 4.7 behind the skylines.
4. **Birds:** blue chevrons drift across the sky with a 9 rad/s flap.
