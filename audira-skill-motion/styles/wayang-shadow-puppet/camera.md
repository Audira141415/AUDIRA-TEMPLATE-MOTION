## 1. Camera Specifications & Optics
- **Rig Type:** **Screen-Edge Orbit Rig** (`getWayangCamera`), orbit centre `[0, 2.0, 0]` (kelir centre).
- **Focal Length:** macro `~50mm` (FOV 30°) on the flame, opening to `~30mm` (FOV 50°), tightening to 46° on the shadow side.
- **Orbit Radius:** 4.9 m, swelling +0.6 m while passing the frame post, closing to 4.2 m for the payoff.
- **Height:** 2.05 m, lifting +0.35 m mid-orbit.

---

## 2. Movement Grammar
- **Flame Hook (0–2.4 s):** start beside the blencong, pull back while the target slides from the flame to the screen.
- **Colour-Side Arc (2.4–5.6 s):** slow drift from 6° to 17° azimuth; puppets perform inside the central ±1.1 m (9:16 is narrow).
- **Orbit Through the Edge (5.6–9.2 s):** ease-in-out 180° swing; the carved post wipes the lens near 7.5 s, puppets and their shadows are seen side by side.
- **Shadow-Side Payoff (9.2–12 s):** small push-in and a last 5° drift while the gunungan closes the scene.
