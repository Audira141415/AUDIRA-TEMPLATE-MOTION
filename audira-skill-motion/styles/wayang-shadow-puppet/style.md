## 1. Aesthetic Essence & Visual Identity
- **Visual Core:** A wayang kulit stage at night. A taut cream kelir screen in a carved teak frame topped by a kawung batik panel, a banana-trunk gedebog holding ornate perforated leather puppets on horn rods, a bronze blencong oil lamp hanging in front. On the dalang side the puppets glow in prada gold, red, green and blue; on the audience side only their sharp lace-like shadows remain on an amber-glowing cloth.
- **Atmosphere:** Ritual, warm, intimate, storytelling by firelight.
- **Narrative Vibe:** Lakon, moral fables, history told like a dalang, heritage and culture explainers.
