# SOUND DESIGN ENGINE: SFX & Foley Catalog
# AUDIRA AUDIO INTELLIGENCE

A high-impact 3D animation relies on precise acoustic events to convince the human brain of physical weight, velocity, and spatial reality.

## 1. Categorized Sound Effect Matrix

### 1.1 Tactile Physical Impacts (Ground Contact & Drops)
| Sound Cue | Frequency Profile | Best Fit Styles | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `impact-wooden-thud.wav` | Low-mid thud (120Hz – 350Hz) | `3d-toy-diorama` | Miniature block or toy foot landing on wooden stage plank |
| `impact-plastic-snap.wav` | High-mid crisp snap (2.5kHz) | `chibi-3d`, `brickcraft` | Interlocking plastic stud clicking into place |
| `impact-clay-squish.wav` | Damped soft plop (80Hz – 200Hz) | `claymation` | Plasticine mass landing softly with squash deformation |
| `impact-metal-clamp.wav` | Heavy metallic slam + ring (800Hz) | `innerworks`, `buildforge` | Heavy structural steel clamp or gear locking shut |
| `impact-porcelain-crack.wav` | High crystalline shatter (4kHz – 8kHz) | `kintsugi-gold` | Fine ceramic hairline fissure or fracture |
